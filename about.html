Who We Are

We're a locally based paving and striping contractor — not dispatched from an hour away, not a national franchise reading from a script. We live with the same heat, wind, and monsoon storms our customers' pavement has to survive, which shapes how we spec every job: material choice, sealcoat timing, and base prep are all built around Coachella Valley conditions specifically, not a generic one-size-fits-all approach.

From a single residential driveway to a full HOA private-road network or a commercial parking lot rebuild, we handle the whole job under one roof: repair, resurfacing, sealcoating, crack filling, striping (including ADA-compliant layouts), and new parking lot paving.
