What's Included

  • Surface prep, crack treatment, and leveling
  • New asphalt overlay application
  • Grading and drainage correction where needed
  • Post-overlay striping coordination

Signs You Need This

  • Widespread cracking across most of the surface
  • Multiple patch repairs that are becoming inefficient
  • Rutting or an uneven, wavy surface
  • A surface that's structurally sound but cosmetically worn

Built for Coachella Valley Conditions

We check drainage and base compaction before any overlay — monsoon flash storms expose bad grading within a single season, and an overlay poured over poor drainage just fails again at the first real storm.
