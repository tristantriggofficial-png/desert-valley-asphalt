Paving in Thousand Palms

Thousand Palms sits along the I-10 corridor between Palm Springs and the rest of the valley, unincorporated and still largely rural in character near the Coachella Valley Preserve and Thousand Palms Oasis — but it's also seeing real industrial and warehouse growth along the freeway frontage.

Wind is the defining factor here — Thousand Palms sits in one of the valley's windiest corridors, and blown sand works into surface cracks and drainage channels faster than almost anywhere else we serve. New industrial and warehouse lots along the I-10 frontage need paving designed for heavy truck traffic from day one, while older rural residential streets need straightforward, honest repair work.

Built for Thousand Palms's Climate

Sand infiltration into cracks and joints is the number one cause of pavement failure we see in Thousand Palms specifically — crack sealing on a shorter cycle than elsewhere in the valley makes a real difference here.
