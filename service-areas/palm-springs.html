Paving in Palm Springs

Palm Springs runs on tourism — hotel and vacation-rental parking lots, downtown retail, and a dense cluster of mid-century modern neighborhoods (Movie Colony, Twin Palms, Warm Sands) where private driveways and small commercial lots both need regular upkeep.

Modernism Week, Pride, and a steady stream of hotel turnover mean Palm Springs parking lots see heavier and more constant traffic than a typical residential-only city — faded striping and curb damage show up fast and get noticed by a lot of visitors. Historic district guidelines in some neighborhoods also mean matching existing material and finish matters more here than elsewhere in the valley.

Built for Palm Springs's Climate

Downtown's open, sun-baked surface lots near the Village and along Palm Canyon Drive age faster than the shaded residential streets of Movie Colony and the Tahquitz Creek area — we sealcoat the former on a tighter cycle than the latter.
