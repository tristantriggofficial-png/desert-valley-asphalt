Pavement Built for the Desert, Not Adapted to It

Every city in the valley shares the same core challenge: extreme summer heat, intense UV exposure, and monsoon flash runoff that breaks down pavement built with generic, one-size-fits-all specs. Being based locally means we see these failure patterns firsthand, city by city, rather than applying a template written for a different climate.

Heat & Oxidation

Triple-digit summer temperatures soften asphalt and speed up oxidation — we schedule sealcoating and repair timing around that, not a generic annual calendar.

UV Degradation

Intense desert UV breaks down binder and fades striping faster here than in milder climates, which is why we use UV-rated sealants and striping paint.

Monsoon Runoff

Summer monsoon storms expose bad grading fast — we check drainage and base compaction before resurfacing, so a new surface survives the first real storm.
