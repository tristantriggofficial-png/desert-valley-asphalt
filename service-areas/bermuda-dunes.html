Paving in Bermuda Dunes

Bermuda Dunes sits between Indio and La Quinta and is unincorporated, which means there's no city road department to call — nearly every street here is privately owned and HOA- or resident-maintained, including the roads around Bermuda Dunes Country Club and the private aviation community near the airport.

Because the county isn't maintaining these roads, HOAs and private owners in Bermuda Dunes carry the full responsibility for pavement condition themselves — which makes a clear, honest estimate and a contractor who'll do a smaller job right (not just chase big commercial contracts) matter more here than in incorporated cities.

Built for Bermuda Dunes's Climate

Open desert exposure on either side of the airport means Bermuda Dunes' private streets see the same heat and UV load as the rest of the valley floor without a city crew keeping an eye on them — regular resident-initiated sealcoating is the only thing protecting these roads long term.
